# The four sellers — the frame for any short

> A short seller's job is to find what will create **selling**. There are four sources of it, and a
> trade wants **at least three of the four**. Not a setup : a way to judge any setup — GUS, double
> top, short into resistance, penny break.

*Last revised : 2026-09-25, from the Trading Desk session of 14 September, « Les Patterns ».*

---

## The four

| # | Seller | Why they sell at the retest | What takes them away |
|---|--------|-----------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Longs in profit** | Entered low, watched the top go by without selling. After a real rejection they sell the next test rather than miss twice. | A rejection too small to convince them the top is in (~10 %). |
| 2 | **Longs at a loss** | Entered near the top, now down 10-20 %. Defensive, not attacking : they want out at break-even, and the retest is where they get it. | Same — no real rejection, no panic. |
| 3 | **Short sellers** | Us : on the sideline, waiting to snipe extended plays. Their entries press the stock down. | **SSR** — uptick-only, so shorts cannot hit the stock at market. Also shares unavailable, or too expensive to borrow. |
| 4 | **Bag holders** | Stuck at a past level with volume — premarket or daily — waiting for the price to come back so they can get out. | No past level, or no volume at it. |

---

## How to use it

Before any short, count the sellers :

- **Where are the longs, and are they in profit or losing right now ?** A 17-20 % rejection is what
  turns 1 and 2 into sellers — this is why the [double top](../pattern/DT.md) asks for it.
- **Can the shorts help ?** Check SSR and the borrow before counting them.
- **Is there a past level with volume** (premarket high, daily resistance) the price is running into ?

Three or four → the story holds, enter with confidence. Two → a red flag, wait for a stronger sign of
weakness or pass.

**Example** — BMGL on 14 September : longs trapped by the morning push and its rejection (1, 2), and a
premarket level at 8.88 as a psychological risk (4) — how much volume sat there is what to check.
On « Stack » in July, SSR removed seller 3 : with a halt level close to 4.40 as well, the double top
was left alone and only a penny break lower down was taken.
