# The stop rule — every pattern

> Once a stop is computed, look around it : a level **1-2 %** further out is worth stretching the
> stop to, and cutting the size to pay for it.

*Last revised : 2026-09-25, from the Trading Desk session of 14 September, « Les Patterns ».*

---

Once the stop is computed, **look around** : daily, hourly, premarket. If a risk sits **1-2 %**
further out — say the stop is at 3.30 and the daily has a level at 3.34 — stretch the stop to it and
**cut the size** to pay for it. The extra level is one more reason for the stock to turn before
taking you out.

1-2 %, not 5-10 : stretching a stop that far is how a robotic plan quietly turns into a hope.

It applies to every pattern : the GUS on a probability entry, the [double top](../pattern/DT.md),
the [short into resistance](../pattern/SIR.md) — whatever computed the stop.
